`webwallgl.global.min.js` is shipped unmodified as the scene/web wallpaper
rendering engine. It is a self-contained classic script exposing
`globalThis.WebWallGL` and inlines its `mediabunny` dependency.

Wallpaper Engine workshop assets (scene.pkg, textures, audio and video) are
not part of this package and remain the property of their respective authors.
